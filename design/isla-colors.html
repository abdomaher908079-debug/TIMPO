Isla: color options

Same app, different colors. Isla herself stays green and orange in all of them.

1. Now

Near-black, orange buttons, neon-green details. Strong, a bit harsh.

2. Midnight

Deep night blue instead of black. Warmer orange and a softer green. Calm and premium; Isla glows like a light at night.

3. Warm studio

Warm dark brown-gray, like a cozy editing room. Orange gradient buttons. Friendly and creative.

4. Clean graphite

Soft dark gray, white buttons, orange only for small details. The most minimal and "big tech"; Isla is the only bright color on screen.

5. Light

Warm off-white, like paper. Orange buttons, darker green for details. Fresh and friendly in daylight; a dark version can be added for night.
