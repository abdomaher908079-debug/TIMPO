Isla: logo concepts

Each one is the logo and the live agent at once. They're animated here the way she would move in the app.

Concept A

The island of sound

Isla

"Isla" means island. The sound bars form an island and the orange sun is Isla. Live: the bars ripple like waves while she listens, and the sun rises when she finds a moment.

Concept B

The dot is Isla

The dot on the "i" is Isla herself, a small glowing orange light. Live: she pulses as she listens, bounces when she finds a moment, and can float off the word to guide you around the app.

Concept C

The orb

Isla

A small glowing orb with eyes, and her voice is the sound wave inside. Live: she blinks, floats, and the bars move like she's talking or listening. The friendliest of the three.
