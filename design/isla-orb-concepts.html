Isla: orb designs

Four looks for Isla's orb. All animated. At the bottom: a demo of her flying around and watching a video being edited.

Design 1

Glass orb

A dark glass sphere with a warm orange light glowing inside and a green-to-orange rim. Modern "assistant" eyes, and a tiny voice wave that moves when she talks or listens.

Design 2

Halo orb

A warm orange core, like a small sun, with a green ring orbiting around her, like the planet of sound. Very recognizable; the ring spins faster when she's working.

Design 3

Living blob

A soft, slowly morphing shape in a green-to-orange gradient, like a friendly living light. The cutest and most emotional; she can squish, stretch and smile.

Design 4

Pure light

Just a glowing ball of warm light with two small eyes and a green aura. The simplest, like a firefly or a spirit; elegant, and the lightest to animate everywhere.
